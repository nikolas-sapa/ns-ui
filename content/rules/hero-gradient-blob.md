## Avoid

A soft radial or elliptical gradient bloom sitting behind the hero headline, usually indigo through cyan or violet, at low opacity so it reads as atmosphere rather than as a shape.

## Instead

One flat field, and let the only luminance step on the screen be the thing you want clicked. Where a page needs depth behind type, site the type where a real image already has no detail instead of bolting a scrim or a bloom over it.

## Why

This is the signal people name first when they say a page looks AI-built, and the evidence only half agrees. It runs at a similar rate in the 2014 control as in current cohorts, so it is not an artifact of generated design; it is an older convention that generated design inherited. What it does track is pages with little else going on: it is absent from every page in the corpus rated exemplar.
