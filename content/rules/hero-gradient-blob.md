## Avoid

Large soft blurred colour shapes behind the hero or a CTA band: discs or washes blurred 40px and up, usually in the accent and one neighbour hue, there to fill space rather than say anything.

## Instead

Let the background be flat, or put something there that carries information: the product, a photograph of the real thing, a diagram. If the page needs warmth, take it from type and one accent, not from fog.

## Why

On every unscreened slop page that settles it and on no exemplar. It is not new: a share of the 2014 control uses soft gradient fields too, which is why the rule is soft. Raters split on it where the blobs barely paint (toppic's six declared blobs move the pixels by a few levels), so faint ones are not the target.
