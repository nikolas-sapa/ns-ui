## Avoid

Every section heading centred over a centred subhead, band after band, so the whole page hangs off one vertical axis. A centred hero is a separate, weaker signal; this is the page that never leaves the axis after it.

## Instead

Set most section headings to a left edge the body copy shares, and centre only where a band is a single statement. Measured as the share of h1/h2 section headings that are centred; the slop pages rated present run at 0.92 or more, the exemplars at 0.71 or less.

## Why

Absent from every exemplar and from the 2014 control studied so far. Soft because the slop pool is small, and because a page that is mostly a single centred statement (a one-screen tool) can trip it without any template behind it.
