## Avoid

Every band on the page centred on the same axis: centred heading, centred subhead, centred button, centred card row, repeated down the full length with no section breaking the pattern.

## Instead

Break the axis at least once, early. An asymmetric split in the hero is the cheapest version; an off-grid element in the second band also works.

## Why

The strongest separator among the slop signals in this corpus, and the one closest to being diagnostic. Centring a single element is not the problem, and the best page in the 2014 control is centred: Mailchimp 2014 sets a 52px headline over a commissioned photographic still life and is centred throughout. What fails is centring everything, so nothing in the page has a reason to be where it is.
