Unequal columns where the hero splits, such as 2:1 or 3:2 between the copy and the product, instead of a 50/50 mirror or a centred stack.

## Why

Common among exemplars and rare among slop. It is an older move than AI-built pages, common in the 2014 control too. Unstudied at phone widths, where most splits collapse to one column.
