## Prefer

Show what the product produces, not the interface that produces it. A priced comparison with real plan names and a refusal in it. A claims ledger with dates and amounts. A force-versus-time chart. A run trace with the actual query in it. The reader should be able to evaluate the output without understanding your UI.

## Why

The strongest discriminator found in this corpus and the one that most changed the vocabulary. It exists because the original signal, real-product-screenshot, meant the opposite thing and had to mark the best pages in the set as deficient: a page showing a priced carrier comparison including two refusals was scoring worse than a page showing a floating dashboard. Absent from every page rated slop and absent from the whole 2014 control, where the convention was a screenshot or an illustration of one.
