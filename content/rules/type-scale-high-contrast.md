## Prefer

Put real distance between the display and the body, around 3:1 or more. A 56-72px headline over 16-17px body reads as a decision; 30px over 17px reads as a default nobody touched.

## Why

Separates exemplars from slop cleanly, and it is the single cheapest change on this list because it costs one number. It is also rising over time: the 2014 control sits well below the current cohorts, which is one of the few places where present-day pages are measurably better than their predecessors rather than merely different.
