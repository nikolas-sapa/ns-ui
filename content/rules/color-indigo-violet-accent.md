## Avoid

Saturated indigo or violet (hue roughly 230° to 290°) as the accent: the button fill, the highlighted word, the active state.

## Instead

Take the accent from something the product or brand actually owns, and use one hue for every accent job. Any hue chosen for a reason beats the kit default.

## Why

The default accent of AI site builders and component kits, and rare among exemplars. It is a weak tell on its own: it predates AI-built pages, with a share of the 2014 control using it, and it fires on a minority of slop pages. Soft for both reasons.
