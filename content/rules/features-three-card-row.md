## Avoid

Features delivered as a row of three equal cards: same width, same icon-heading-sentence stack, same border or shadow, one row, as if the product had exactly three things to say.

## Instead

Size the features by how much each matters: one dominant feature with detail, a list, a table, a product screen with callouts. If there really are three, let them differ in weight or form.
