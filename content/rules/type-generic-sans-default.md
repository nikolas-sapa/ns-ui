## Avoid

Every heading and body line in the framework's shipped sans (Inter, Geist or the system stack), untuned, with no second voice anywhere on the page.

## Instead

Choose a face for the job, or keep the default and earn the hierarchy with a real display size, deliberate tracking and weight. A serif or monospace display over a sans body does this cleanly.

## Why

A default sans is not a defect on its own, and some exemplar pages use one well, which is why the rule is soft. It separates because it travels with pages that made no other typographic decision either.
