## Avoid

Body and display set in the same unmodified default sans, usually the framework's shipped Inter or a system stack, at one weight and one scale, with no second voice anywhere on the page.

## Instead

Pair two faces with a job each, or stay in one face and earn the hierarchy from scale and weight. A monospace display over a sans body does this as cleanly as a serif does.

## Why

Using a default sans is not itself a defect, and one page rated exemplar in this corpus does it. It separates because it travels with pages that made no other typographic decision either, which is why the rule is soft: it is a symptom worth checking, not a verdict.
