## Avoid

Social proof delivered as three or more equal-weight quote cards in a row, as the whole section: avatar, name, stars, one generic sentence each.

## Instead

One quote with a named, checkable source and enough context to be specific, a single customer story, or a number the reader can verify. Fewer, heavier proofs read as real; a uniform row reads as filled in.
